Need help right now?

If you are in distress, please reach out right away. In the US, call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line). This site is peer advice only, not professional medical, legal, or crisis help.

Ask a question

Medical and health questions are welcome. This is peer advice, not professional medical advice — please see a doctor or professional for medical decisions.

House rules

  • Be kind. Advice only — no judging, arguing, or put-downs.
  • Anonymous only: no real names, no pictures, no private messages.
  • No rude or abusive comments — they are blocked before they post.
  • No hardcore or explicit sexual questions or posts. Nasty or rude sexual posts are not allowed.
  • Medical-type questions are fine, with the peer-advice note above.
  • Use Report on any post or reply that breaks the rules — it is hidden right away for review.
